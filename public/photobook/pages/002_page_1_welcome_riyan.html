Welcome Riyan!

07 January 2023

Dear Riyan,

Today is the 7th of January 2023. For a long time we were waiting for this day. Early in the morning I woke up and came to know that your Mom Nidhi was uncomfortable and had bouts of pain. Though we were not sure whether it was labour pain or otherwise, we decided to take her to the hospital Cooley Dickinson where your Mumma had already registered herself for the child birth. Your Papa as he is, you know, immediately planned everything, put all essentials in the car and got ready. I too made some food arrangements for your Dadu and accompanied your Mom and Dad. All packed up in thick jackets, around 4 in the morning we drove through the deserted roads to reach the hospital in time. Nurses and doctors on duty examined your mom, admitted her and planned some procedures. Going through a lot of pain and a tough time, at 6 in the evening you were born. The nurse first put you on your mom's tummy and then gave you in your dad's and in my hands. You were so pretty, so cute, so lovely — we all were overwhelmed to see you. Papa called Neha Masi who came to your 5 Kensington house and drove your Dadu to the hospital to meet you. After looking at your sleeping face both of us returned home. Your Papa stayed in the hospital to take care of your mom and of you who were very delicate and fragile. Always remember one thing my little Prince — your Mom and Dad are the two people who love you the most. Love them equally when you grow up.

Many blessings and lots of love,
Dadi
