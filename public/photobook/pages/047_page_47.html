Falling down from sofa
26 December 2023

Dear Riyan,

Today was a very difficult day. Early in the morning you woke up playful and happy. When your Papa video called us you were playing with your favourite toys. Your Nanu, Masi Mumma and Papa were all around. Your Dadu and me too were enjoying your little lovable activities of saying Hi and Bye.

After an hour of our switching off from the call, your Papa informed us that you fell down from the big room sofa and little blood oozed out from your nose. Aman and Nidhi were taking you to the emergency. Your Dadu and me got so upset and kept praying to God that our little precious Riyan should be absolutely fine. Nurse thoroughly checked you and found you absolutely ok. Doctor too confirmed it and sent all of you home.

My dear little Nona Puttu these incidents warn us to be more cautious and to keep faith in God.

Lots of love, Dadi
