Aditi Aunty, Karan Uncle Visit

18 February 2023

Dear Riyan,

Your Mumma and Papa have very close friends living in New Jersey — Aditi aunty, Karan uncle and their daughters Sara and Ahana. They are a very warm and loving family. They wanted to meet you and your Dadu and me before our going back to India. Since long they were trying to visit but due to certain health reasons could not finalise a trip. They stayed with us overnight and we all had lots of fun. Your Nanima met them for the first time but she too loved them.

Sara and Ahana wanted to take you in their lap and play with you. You are just one month old but still you could respond well to everyone's call.

My dear cutie pie, a strong bond of love with family and friends is very important for our existence.

Love,
Dadi
