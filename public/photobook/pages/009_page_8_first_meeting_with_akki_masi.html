First Meeting with Akki Masi

22 January 2023

Dear Riyan,

Today your Akki Masi came to meet you. She was in India, visiting her parents when you were born. A few days ago she reached back and was so excited to see you. Your mom and dad asked her to quarantine herself for some time as she had travelled a long way and you are still so small and delicate. Finally, after taking some clinical health tests, she is here to meet you — to take you in her hands for the first time.

Neha Masi and Akki Masi are both your Mom's best friends and are an integral part of the family. They love you so much. You are an adorable child — not only do our family and friends love you, but people who meet you for the first time love you too.

God bless you my little cutie pie,
Dadi
