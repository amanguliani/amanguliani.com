Chaa… means Peekaboo

25 May 2024

Dear Riyan,

Last week you were playing with your Dadu and having a fun time. Dada covered his eyes with both hands and then removing them said chaa. It means peekaboo in Punjabi. You loved this game and repeated it many times. Yesterday we all went to a coffee shop. You went to all the tables and played this game with the people sitting there. Residents of America do not understand our local language — so Aman had to explain them that peekaboo is called chaa in our language. It was so amazing to see you playing chaa with one and all.

Love you my naughty boy,
Dadi
