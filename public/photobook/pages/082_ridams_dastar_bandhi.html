Ridam's dastar bandhi

29 December 2024

Dear Riyan,

Your Ridam Bhaiya tied his turban today in Gurudwara Sahib. Sikh families celebrate this event and call it the dastar bandhi function. Your Bua wanted you and your Mom and Dad to be a part of this special day — so when the plan of visiting LA was finalised, she arranged the function details accordingly. I got updates through pictures and videos. You all are looking great wearing formal and ethnic attire. You are very small and do not know the significance of this day but you looked so excited and kept moving around your big brother. When I visit you next I will bring some books of Sikh history and will ask your Papa to read them to you. My dear cutie pie, you too will tie a turban when you grow up.

Love you a lot,
Dadi
