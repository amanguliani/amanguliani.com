Tasting sugar and spice
10 January 2024

Dear Riyan,

After turning one you have started showing many different interests and tastes. Your Mom has been very particular that you should not be given salt and sugar till one year, so you didn't know the taste of salty or spicy food. One day you saw your Neha Masi eating aloo bhujia and other people eating Parle G biscuits. They offered you too a little bite. Now these are your favourite foods!

You know where in the pantry they are kept. With your finger you point towards that side and want your favourite food. If by chance a bit of bhujia fell on the ground... you don't want to waste it... you will lie down on the ground and pick that piece with your tongue.

Cutie pie, your these activities we love.

Dadi
