Manraj Bua's Arrival

08 January 2023

Dear Riyan,

This morning we woke up with a knock at the door. Your Manraj Bua from LA landed at Hartford airport, rented a car and reached home. After serving her some breakfast we all got ready to reach the hospital and to see our one day old prince. You are still very small but you seemed a little better settled today. Bua took you in her hands for the first time. The doctor informed that your mom and you will be discharged from the hospital the next day. After doing our regular grocery for the week we came home to prepare for the special day when you are coming home. Neha Masi, Surya Chachu and your Bua inflated so many blue and white balloons and decorated stairs and entire pathway to your bedroom. I decorated your basinet with the greeting card I brought from India and with the print out of my poem which I wrote for you.

My little darling always remember that your bua is the only sibling your papa has. She and her family love you so much. Neha Masi and Surya Chachu are the two most loving and trust worthy friends your Mumma and Papa have. They all are family to you.

Many hugs (no kisses for now…I am concerned for your delicate skin),
Dadi
