First Father's Day

18 June 2023

Dear Riyan,

Today is Father's Day — a very special day for your Papa. He is celebrating this day as a father for the first time. Your Mumma gifted him and you both a T-shirt marking the significance of this day. Also she got customised a picture frame of both of you.

Your old Nanny Janette is leaving due to certain personal reasons. Fiona your new Nanny has joined to take care of you — she is a young and very affectionate person.

Now you are able to sit for a while and show strong reflexes. Today we saw you crawling with your head down (froggy pose) on your favourite gadda — a small mattress made with cotton filling. It was an amazing scene. Normally children crawl when they are seven months old. You have proved your doctor's judgement true that you are ahead in your developmental milestones. May God bless you always.

Love,
Dadi
