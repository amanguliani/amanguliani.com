Tum tum to

28 July 2024

Dear Riyan,

Summer on the east coast is the time when one can enjoy it the maximum. My passion to grow plants gets satisfied in this season only. I bring saplings from the nursery and grow them in the pots lying in your backyard. My tomatoes and bell pepper plants are blooming with flowers and fruits these days. Whenever I went to the backyard I showed your Mom Dad these tomatoes and bell peppers. You must have been observing it. One day playing in the backyard you touched one of the fruits and said “Da… tum tum to.” It took me a minute to realise that you want to tell me that this is the tomato. We all had a hearty laugh over this cute little incident and enjoyed listening to “Tum Tum To”. My dear, all kids of your age pronounce many words in different ways. It is all natural, but we grown-ups enjoy these cute little talks of our dear ones.

Love,
Dadi
