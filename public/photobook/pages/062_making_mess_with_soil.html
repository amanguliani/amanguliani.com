Making mess with soil

21 April 2024

Dear Riyan,

Month of April is very cold in your area and not the right time to grow vegetation. Plant lover as I am, I wanted to make advance preparation so I planted few vegetable seeds in the soil in a pot and placed it near the window in the dining room. Playing with soil always fascinates children of your age. You walked towards the pot, dug the entire soil, spread it over the floor and thoroughly enjoyed the act. Pictures show how contented you were after this game. Nidhi and me cleared the total mess and I removed that pot to the garage. You kept narrating this glorious story again and again. My dear, it's good to be connected to your soil. I am sure you will love gardening when you grow up.

Love,
Dadi
