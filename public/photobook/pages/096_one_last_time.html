One last time (1 May 2025)

Dear Riyan,

You know that Dadi is fond of her plants…so you pick up your spray bottle and will ask…Dadi kaun sa plant dry hai…you touch the soil and decide isko pani chahiye. Nature attracts you so much. You read the book Bloom Boom every day, take a round of your outdoor premises and see tulips growing.

Realising your intense love for nature, Aman has made a film of tulips growing from their bulbs by using artificial intelligence. Every morning when you go to your Papa’s office to drop him there for his meeting you ask him to show that film to you. You are not satisfied by watching it once….your favourite sayings…one last time…one final last time…goes on and on.

We all feel good to see your varied interests and obsessively following them.

Love you little one,
Dadi
