Papa and You Slipping from Staircase

11 February 2023

Dear Riyan,

After celebrating your first month birthday, we are all enjoying your little giggles and playful activities. Your Mom Nidhi too is gradually gaining strength and is able to take care of you besides doing her other daily chores. Your Papa is a big support — he takes care of the entire family.

Today late evening we had an accident which involved you and your Papa. We all were in the kitchen making dinner when your Papa, who was coming down from upstairs, slipped a few steps and fell down. He tightly held you but got his own back hurt. The incident shook all of us deeply. We are thankful to God that He saved both of you.

My dear most precious little one, I wish and pray to God that He should always keep you safe and protect you from all sorts of unpleasant happenings.

Love,
Dadi
