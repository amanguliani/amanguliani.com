Met Jasbir Sandhu Dadu and cousin Honey

19 July 2023

Dear Riyan,

Last week has been a very hectic one. As we were packing our bags to go back to India, Dadu's college friend Jasbir Sandhu and his wife Manjit wanted to visit us. They reached here on 15th and were with us for two days. Then came your second cousin Honey and his wife Tanya — yes, a big cousin of yours! Sanjeev Dadu and family are also a very close friend of ours. We are meeting them somewhere near Connecticut for lunch. Aman will drive us there.

You are too small for such a long drive so you will remain home with your Mom. It is really difficult to leave you my little darling and go back to India but your Dadu has his official commitments and I cannot leave him alone for a long time. I will keep writing to you from there and very soon will visit you again.

Love,
Dadi
