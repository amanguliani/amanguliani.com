Appointment with ENT

13 January 2023

Dear Riyan,

As I mentioned in my previous letter, you had an appointment with your ENT doctor today. Though it appeared to be a simple procedure, still I decided to accompany your parents to the clinic. I was concerned because you are just five days old and very delicate. Clinic people did not allow me to go inside with your Mumma and Papa. Sitting in the waiting hall I was praying to Waheguru that everything should go well. Your Papa kept us updated on our WhatsApp group.

Everything was going well — doctor put a cut and then tried to check it by putting her finger under your tongue. Suddenly you started bleeding from the place where she had put the cut. Your Mumma started crying bitterly. Your Papa too got tensed up. Your Dadu and me were also worried. Finally doctor could stop the bleeding, prescribed a few medicines, and we all came home. You are in pain and unable to eat. We have decided to celebrate your Lohri tomorrow on 14th.

My dear little one, you are too precious for all of us. May God bless you with a long and healthy life.

Lots of love,
Dadi
