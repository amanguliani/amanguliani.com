Beard in the belly

5 January 2025

Dear Riyan,

Your early understanding of your surroundings and ability to repeat things made us so happy that we jokingly started saying that Riyan has a “dari” (beard) in his tummy. The literal meaning of this saying is that you are a very intelligent boy and you know a lot. Today when I was video chatting with you, I was so amused to see you demonstrating the entire process — saying that you have a “dari” in your belly, and when you grow up it will come out, and by touching your cheeks you showed that it will come here. “Dari Riyan ki belly main hai… Riyan big boy ho jayega… fir bahar aa jayegi.” My dear little one, it is a real delight to see you talking in both Hindi and English.

Love you,
Dadi
