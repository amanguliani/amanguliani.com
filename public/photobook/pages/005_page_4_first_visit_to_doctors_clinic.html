First Visit to Doctor's Clinic

11 January 2023

Dear Riyan,

Today is your first visit to the pediatrician. It is very cold outside and you are too tiny. Your Mumma dressed you up in warm clothes and your Papa belted you up in your car seat wrapping you in a thick blanket. The doctor examined you thoroughly, compared your present body weight with your birth weight and was a little concerned. She wanted to see you again the very next day. She told Mumma Papa not to worry if you are eating and sleeping well.

My little prince, health is the greatest wealth you will need your whole life. May you grow up to be a healthy and charming young man.

Love,
Dadi
