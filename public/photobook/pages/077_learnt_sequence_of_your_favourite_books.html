Learnt sequence of your favourite books

19 December 2024

Dear Riyan,

Today morning I got a call from your Papa. Overwhelmed with your complete day’s activities, he shared your sweet titbits with me. I immediately decided to record them and send you an email. You are a keen observer and a quick learner. You learn things in proper sequence and want others to repeat them in the same manner. You wanted Aman to read your favourite book “My Home” which carries pictures of all the articles used in our houses. You were enjoying each page rather than jumping ahead — when by mistake your Papa missed one of your most liked articles, the “happy watering can”, you annoyingly stopped participating further in the book reading and wanted Aman to go back to that page.

In another incident your Papa deliberately missed the part where Peppa was told she is big enough and cannot sit in the shopping cart — as it was your nap time. Here also you astonished your father by asking him why he had missed that incident. My dear little one, a teacher, psychologist and grandmother in me can sense that God has gifted our family with an intelligent child. Blessed are we.

Love,
Dadi
