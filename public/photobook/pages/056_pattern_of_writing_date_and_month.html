Pattern of writing date and month

25 March 2024

Dear Riyan,

You might get astonished over the fact that all the dates on the emails posted to you have date first and month later on. This is of course a different system than the one normally Americans follow. They write month and then date. In India we have been taught to write date this way. Your Mumma, Papa and your Bua and Fufu, were all raised in India but after living in US for so many years they learnt and got accustomed to American style of writing the date. Many other things like car driving, measurement of temperature, length and weight are different in America. You have to follow the system of the country you live in but should also understand the concept which is been followed elsewhere.

Love,
Dadi
