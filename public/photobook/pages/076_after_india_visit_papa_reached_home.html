After India visit Papa reached home

15 December 2024

Dear Riyan,

Your Papa reached back home yesterday evening after coming from India. He had a hectic fortnight in which he had to take care of me at the time of my surgery and then attend official meetings and eMBA classes. In the evening you were awake and just ready to meet your Papa. He brought a blue car on your demand, and stuffies of Peppa and Georgie which are your favourite fictional characters. You loved everything Papa brought for you. Today morning you again had a great time with your dad over sips of tea, rusk bites and pieces of bread. At the time of your afternoon nap you spent 35 minutes in the crib singing “yeh meri chand baliyan…” When Papa went to pick you up you said “I had a good nap” — which made your Mom and Dad laugh! Later in the evening when this incident was narrated to us we too had a hearty laugh.

Love,
Dadi
