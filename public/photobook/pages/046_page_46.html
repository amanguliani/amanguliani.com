First Christmas celebration
24 December 2023

Dear Riyan,

Today is your first Christmas eve. Living in a country where majority people follow Christianity, Christmas is a big festival. I truly believe that these festivals bring people closer to each other. Connected by internet, world is a global village now — Diwali is celebrated in the US also and we Indians enjoy Christmas thoroughly.

Neha, Surya, Akki, Aman, Nidhi and your Nanu ordered similar type of Pajamas and you were made to wear the red dress of Santa Claus. A big Christmas tree is standing tall in your family room all decorated with lights, bells, balls and other buntings. Every time you go near the tree you pull one of its decorative items and then ask your Papa to tie it again.

My dear little one I have found you to be a very loving child who wants family and friends together. Always be so loving.

Love you, Dadi
