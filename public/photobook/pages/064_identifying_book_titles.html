Identifying book titles

5 May 2024

Dear Riyan,

Your Mom Dad bought you so many board books from day one. You too enjoyed reading books, looking at the pictures and associating them with the real world. Mumma, Papa, Fiona and me loved reading books to you. Chicka Chicka Boom Boom is your all time favourite, but you loved reading other books too — showing colours, objects, helpers, etc. Suddenly we observed that you have started identifying all the books with their titles. It is so amazing when we ask you to get a particular book — you go towards your book shelf and get us the exact book we asked for. You are a smart kid, my dear. Many blessings to you.

Love,
Dadi
