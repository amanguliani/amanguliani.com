Welcome to the automated world

11 September 2024

Dear Riyan,

Yesterday evening when Aman video called us to chat with you, I saw you climbing the stairs and going to your bedroom. You got into your crib, picked up each of your stuffy friends by name — Marwin, Melony, Sterny, Bruno and many more. First you decorated them on the edge of your crib, then threw them on the floor, got down from your crib and announced “Riyan betho!” and played with all of them. Then you tiptoed towards the guest bedroom and gave the command: “Hey Google, turn light…” — though Google did not respond to your soft and sweet voice, I was so happy to see you getting acquainted with the technology. Welcome to the automated world, my little one.

Love you,
Dadi
