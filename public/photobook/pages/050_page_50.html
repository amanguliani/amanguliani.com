Sleep coaching
24 January 2024

Dear Riyan,

Your parents were worried and concerned about your erratic sleep cycle. Sometimes you slept in between your Mom Dad on their bed. Sometimes you wanted to cling to your Mom or Dad the whole night. This disturbed theirs and yours — not only night but day also.

Finally your Dad consulted a child sleep coach who suggested that you should sleep in your crib with your favourite stuffies. It took a few days to set your routine but finally you adopted the system and started sleeping holding any one of your stuffies. Later on it was a regular practice two times a day — any one of your Mom Dad or Nanny took you to your Mom Dad's bedroom and left you in your crib with all your stuffies. Lights were switched off, curtains were put, sound machine was switched on and they left the room.

Outside from the camera fixed on your crib they kept observing you. Firstly you played and talked to your friends, then gradually you lay down and slept. Being a smart kid, you could make out where the camera was!

Love, Dadi
