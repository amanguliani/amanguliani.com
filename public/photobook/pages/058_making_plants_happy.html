Making plants happy

2 April 2024

Dear Riyan,

Your Mom loves gardening and she has decorated your house with many indoor plants. Here in this area where you live-in it snows 4 to 5 months of the year. Plants require very less water when kept inside the house. Your Mom waters them once a week. When your Dadu and me reached here this time, in the month of March, Nidhi delegated this duty of watering the plants to me. The very first day I started watering the plants holding you in my arms. I told you that plants are happy now and are thanking you. From the very next day you wanted to continue with the process again and again. Finally to save your Mom's plants from being over watered we started hiding the water can telling you that Katty has taken it from window and Dadu will go and snatch it from her. My dear little one I am sure you will grow up to be a great lover of nature. Will protect it and will help making our mother earth better.

Lots of love,
Dadi
