Motor, Pulley and Belt (10 Apr 2025)

Dear Riyan,

You have a great interest in scientific and technological things. A screwdriver is your favourite toy. Your Mom and Dad, looking at your interests, get you toy models of all the regular machines available in the house. You have a blender, a dough-making machine, a coffee maker, a toaster, and many more.

Today, you were tired and did not want to eat. To engage you in an activity and to feed you, your Papa took you to the basement, unplugged the treadmill, unscrewed its cover and showed you its motor, its pulley and its belt….within no time you learnt the process of electric energy supply to the machine…moving its motor then pulley and then belt. The double arrangement to give your body some energy and satisfy your mental inquisitiveness was accomplished.

The concepts were registered in your brain permanently. You kept repeating…pahele motor chalti hai fir pulley aur fir belt.

My dear little one, you always astonish me with your quick understanding and memory.

Love,
Dadi
