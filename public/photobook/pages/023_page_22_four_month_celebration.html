Four month celebration

07 May 2023

Dear Riyan,

Today you are four months old. Your birthday celebration is planned in the same manner — dressing you up, marking 4 months milestone on the sheet your Mumma Papa has kept for each month, cake cutting and lots of hugs and kisses from your Neha Masi, Surya Chachu, Akansha Masi, Dadi and of course from your parents.

This time we had an additional guest. One of Akki Masi's friend Kripani wanted to visit you. She too loved you a lot and had fun time with you. I was overwhelmed to see you sitting in my lap, cutting cake and very confidently holding your head.

My dear little one I wish that Baba ji bless you always and you have a long and healthy life.

Blessings,
Dadi
