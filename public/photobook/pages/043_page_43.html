Diwali celebration
19 November 2023

Dear Riyan,

Diwali is a great Indian festival. In India we celebrate Diwali with great pomp and show as Christmas is celebrated in the western world. A week long celebrations are held before and after the day this festival falls. It's your first Diwali this year. Your Mom's Grad School friends Chanchal and Sanjana live in Boston. They invited you to come to their house for the festival celebration. Later coming back to 5 Kensington Drive a grand Diwali party was organised with the same old friends Neha, Surya, Akansha, Nidhi and some of your Mom's Lab Mates. Dressed up in an ethnic attire everyone lit Diyas and candles, burnt crackers and had a grand party.

Festivals give us pleasure and joy, make us socialize with people and we say good bye to our monotonous and stressful life for a short while. My dear Riyan the thumb rule to today's hard life is to take small breaks whenever needed and enjoy those moments to the fullest.

Lots of love, Dadi
