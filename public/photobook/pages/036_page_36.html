Cruising on sofa, plucking leaves
10 September 2023

Dear Riyan,

With every passing day you are becoming active and putting your parents on their toes. You have started climbing stairs. One elder person needs to be with you to keep watch on you. If left on sofa you start cruising it from one corner to another. Aman keeps running after you so that you should not fall.

Another activity of yours which makes Nidhi and Aman alert is that you pluck leaves of all the plants around and start throwing them on the floor. Nidhi has to put all the plants up so that they are out of your reach. My dear Riyan plants are important source of our living. They provide us with food and above all oxygen for us to breathe. Your parents keep teaching you these important facts of life.

My dear when you grow up, be a nature friendly person and protect your environment and mother earth.

Love, Dadi
