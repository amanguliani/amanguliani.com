Introduction to Riyan’s book

It has always been my passion to spend time with the children and to enjoy each and every moment spent with them. I believe that every child is different, therefore the parenting and grandparenting should also differ depending upon a child’s personality.

Riyan is my first grandchild born to Aman and Nidhi and third in a row after Ridam and Reet my daughter Manraj’s children. Reet is different from Ridam and Riyan is different from both Ridam and Reet. To find out genetic streaks and associate physical and behavioral characters with Mom — Dad, Grand Mas — Grand Pas, Aunts and cousins is an integral part of our Parenting. In Riyan’s case also we thoroughly enjoyed this streaking. Starting from his pattern of sleeping to his food tastes, his way of standing, his habit of eating and smiling all resembled someone or the other in the family. My little darling is still struggling hard to be an original individual.

I thoroughly enjoyed all such conversations and penned them down. I am sure Riyan will enjoy reading them when he will grow up. Many blessings, wishes and lots of love to all the children and grandchildren in this world.

Ravinder Guliani (A Grand Ma first and a Mom later)
