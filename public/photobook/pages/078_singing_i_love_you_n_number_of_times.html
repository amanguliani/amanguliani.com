Singing "I love you" n number of times

20 December 2024

Dear Riyan,

After visiting your Dadu and me, Aman has returned back to the US. You missed him so much and now you don’t want to leave him even for a single minute. You wanted to get the maximum of his time and attention. Early in the morning you want Papa to bring milk for you, and then Papa’s bed and his lap make you happy. Then you go to Papa’s study and help him start his meeting. In the evening after splashy time you hug your old Dinu, New Dinu, newly brought Peppa and Georgie, and give a long list of songs to your Papa which he needs to sing in the sequence given by you — “I Love You”, “Pasoori” and “Baby Beluga”. Every time the list changes and so does its repetition. Sometimes poor Aman has to sing “I Love You” n number of times! I am happy, Riyan, that you love your Mom and Dad a lot — and they too love you so much.

Love you my Nona Puttu,
Dadi
