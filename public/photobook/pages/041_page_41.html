Halloween celebration
31 October 2023

Dear Riyan,

Every year this day is celebrated as Halloween in the US. People decorate their houses with carved pumpkins and children dress up in different characters and go for trick or treat. It is your 1st Halloween. It was decided that you will dress up to be Thor and rest all adults including your Mom Dad and Masi Chachu will dress up wearing Marvel characters.

Your Mom Dad planned a photo shoot on 28th. This included your pictures in the fall season. On 29th your parents took you for a t-shirt choice. Coloured t-shirts of Harry Potter's 4 different houses — Gryffindor, Ravenclaw, Hufflepuff and Slytherin — were put at a distance in front of you. They wanted to know which one is your favourite house. You crawled towards the red t-shirt which is Gryffindor!

My dear little one, festivities of different places have their unique significance. When you will grow up your parents will update you with the information relating to this land.

Lots of love, Dadi
