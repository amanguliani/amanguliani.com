Keen observation
21 March 2024

Dear Riyan,

You are a keen observer — this I knew from the very beginning. Today you proved that. Two little planters were lying on the dining room window. You could reach those plants and off and on wanted to touch them. They are ceramic pots and could be unsafe for you.

Keeping in mind your safety I thought of transferring those pots to the window in your living room, where I thought you could not reach. Your eyes followed my movement and within a second you were there touching those pots.

I love your these activities my little naughty darling.

Dadi
