Eleven months old
07 December 2023

Dear Riyan,

You turned eleven months old today. Your special day today could not be celebrated with great pomp and show as your Papa is still COVID positive. Both your Masis and your Chachu were strictly instructed by your Mom Dad to keep distance as this is a highly contagious disease.

When I video chatted with you I was amused to see you enjoying presence of your Mumma Papa and Fiona. Mumma made a small aloo paranthi (a potato filled wheat roti) and a sabzi of peas and potatoes. She was feeding you small bites and I thoroughly enjoyed the beautiful scene.

Later Papa uploaded few pics in which I can clearly see your one small newly erupted tooth. My little one, may God keep you safe and healthy and may you always enjoy company of your dear ones.

Dadi
