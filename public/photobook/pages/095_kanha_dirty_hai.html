Kanha dirty hai (26 Apr 2025)

Dear Riyan,

Cleaning is an obsession with both your parents. They always keep your house neat and tidy. You too have picked up this habit to clean your surroundings. When we reached here this time I saw that you have a toy vacuum, a toy blower and along with your Papa you are always on the task of cleaning.

Few days ago Aman bought you a real blower which is not very heavy. This blue colored blower is your favourite. With great effort you hold it and start questioning me — Dadi batao kanha dirty hai. I have to tell you Riyan carpet dirty hai…sofa dirty hai.

Next you pick up a water spraying bottle and start flowing water on the window pane or on the glass of the door…then you tell me Dadi ise cloth se dry kar do nahi to nissan par jayenge.

A little boy growing up among so many adults has picked up phrases spoken by adults…but they appear so cute when uttered by a 2 year old child.

Many hugs and kisses,
Dadi
