Narrating Peppa story

2 January 2025

Dear Riyan,

You read many books and listen to stories again and again. Last summer Bua gave you a story book of Peppa Pig. It has long stories related to Peppa and Georgie. When Papa came to India for my surgery he bought two beautiful stuffed toys of Peppa and Georgie — they are your favourite stuffies. You ask your Mumma and Papa to read these stories to you every day. Now you have learnt them by heart. Today you were sitting in your crib and narrating one of the stories in a proper sequence. My dear, you are like your cousin Ridam — he too is an avid reader. Always be like this, my little darling.

Love,
Dadi
