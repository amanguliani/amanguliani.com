Nanu worried about berry-stained lips
22 September 2023

Dear Riyan,

Your Mumma always planned a healthy and nutritious diet for you. You liked banana, oranges and watermelon but did not relish berries. To develop your taste she made a puree of these berries and started feeding you. Puree of blue berries makes your tongue and lips blood red.

A strange incident occurred today. Papa uploaded one of your pictures on the group with red stains on your face. Your Nana was in India... he saw your picture and thought that probably you got hurt and had blood on the face. Worriedly he called me. Incidentally I had a video chat with you and knew that no such incident has happened. I pacified him and assured him that our little darling is fine.

My dear you are lucky that you have a family which loves you a lot.

Dadi
