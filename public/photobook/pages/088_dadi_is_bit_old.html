Dadi is bit old (13 Mar 2025)

Dear Riyan,

After travelling from India we reached here last night when you were fast asleep. Early in the morning your Mumma brought you down….you immediately came in my arms and asked about Dadu too. Looking at your lovely face and your cute activities I decided to add few more pages to your book.

You presumed that after my knee surgery I would be able to sit down on the ground with you but when I told you that I will play and run with you but will not be able to sit on the ground you looked satisfied. Then you wanted me to come to your room and meet with all your stuffy friends lying in the Crib.

While climbing stairs you kept talking to yourself that Dadi is an adult but a bit old. I tried to hide my smile on your understanding of all facts with great clarity.

Love,
Dadi
