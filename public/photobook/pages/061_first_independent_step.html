First independent step

18 April 2024

Dear Riyan,

Since last few weeks you were trying hard to walk on your own but after a couple of steps you looked for support. Holding just one finger of your Dadu or mine you could walk quite a distance. We all were eagerly waiting when you will walk on your own — totally independent. Today morning Nidhi removed the mat lying on the carpet in the big room. Aman was in New York. Dadu and I were sitting on the chairs and you were playing with your Mom on the carpet. Suddenly you got up and started walking on your own — very confidently and independently. We were so happy to see you completely biped! Whole day you practised your newly acquired skill. In the evening Aman was also informed of your new milestone. I immediately captured this important moment with my camera and with my pen.

Stay blessed my dear cutie pie,
Dadi
