Favourite Stuffies

27 November 2024

Dear Riyan,

I have already mentioned in my previous letters that you talk and play with your stuffies lying in your crib before your sleep time. Your favourites are Dinu and New Dinu. After playing with all your stuffed toys you keep these two inside the crib and throw the rest outside one by one. Every day your Mumma and Papa watch this activity on the monitor connected to the camera fixed on the crib. Georgie is the stuffed toy Fiona gave you as a parting gift. Nowadays Georgie has been added to your favourites. During your afternoon nap there is a dim light around which helps you to pick up these three favourites, but at night you touch and identify Dinu and Georgie, keep them aside, and throw the rest outside. Then, tightly hugging them, you go to sleep.

My dear little one, I am writing these precious moments so that you can enjoy them when you grow up.

Love,
Dadi
