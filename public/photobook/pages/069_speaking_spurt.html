Speaking spurt

22 September 2024

Dear Riyan,

Since last week I am checking your videos on our Google group and finding that you are on a spurt to speak out everything you have learnt by this time. You repeat all the poems, songs, stories and small games you have been told by your Mummy, Papa, your Dadu and me and your Nannies — in English or in Hindi-Punjabi. You not only repeat their ending words but also the complete sentences in proper sequence. You remember the complete story books you have been listening to since you were a baby. “Machli jal ki rani hai”, Llama Llama Red Pajama, Twinkle Twinkle Little Star… everything. Today you told me “Akki masi tomorrow aaegi.” It is a real pleasure to listen to you speaking these things in your not very clear — or “totli” — voice. It’s really amazing that you are learning so fast. May God always bless you, my cutie pie. May you always excel in all the fields.

Love,
Dadi
