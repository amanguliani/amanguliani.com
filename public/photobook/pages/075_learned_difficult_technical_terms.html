10 December 2024

Dear Riyan,

With every passing day you are able to speak more clearly. When Nidhi saw you speaking difficult words with great clarity she, being a scientist, decided to teach you a few technical terms. One day she told you that the technique Akki Masi is using for her research is the Mass Spectrometer. You learned it within no time. In another incident she taught you how she makes yoghurt at home: heat the milk, cool it, and inoculate it. You learnt to repeat the entire process.

One day you came to your Mom and told her that the brown kitty sitting in the brown leaves is getting camouflaged. There is a long list of such words and techniques — Homo Sapiens, Fret Sensor, DNA Helix and the complete digestive system — which you could learn. So proud of you, Riyan. My dear cutie pie, may you keep learning things in the same manner and always make your parents happy.

Love,
Dadi
