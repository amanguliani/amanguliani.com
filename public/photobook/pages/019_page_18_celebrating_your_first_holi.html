Celebrating your first Holi

13 March 2023

Dear Riyan,

Yesterday was the festival of Holi. In India we celebrate this festival with great fervor. This day signifies victory over evil. People put colors to all their friends and loved ones, sing, dance and offer sweets. Gujiya is an Indian sweet which people eat on this day.

You have also celebrated Holi with both of your Masis Neha and Akansha, your Chachu Surya and your Nani. Your Mumma and Papa were of course part of the celebration. They all put colors on your dress.

These colors contain chemicals and should not be put on your skin as you are too small and your skin is very delicate. My dear little one festivities are good for social get togethers and to bring happiness in one's life but the present form of celebrations have many health hazards which should be avoided.

Love,
Dadi
