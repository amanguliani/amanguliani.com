Celebrating First Lohri

14 January 2023

Dear Riyan,

Last night was a sleepless and painful one for all of us. You were unable to take feed because of the cut under your tongue. We all were worried and concerned because milk is the only source to give you energy and keep you hydrated. As I have already mentioned, you are a fighter. Finally in the morning you felt a little better and had feed from your Mumma.

Today is Sangrand, the first day of our Indian calendar — an auspicious day that falls immediately after Lohri. We decided to celebrate your first Lohri today. Papa invited Neha Masi, Surya Chachu and Nidhi Bua for dinner. Arrangements for a bonfire and some popcorns and peanuts to be thrown in it had already been done. Evening was all good. We celebrated your first Lohri in a simple but ritualistic manner.

My dear little one, may you celebrate many many more festivals in your long, healthy and happy life.

Many blessings,
Dadi
