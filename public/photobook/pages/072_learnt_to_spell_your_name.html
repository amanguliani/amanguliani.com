Learnt to spell your name

25 October 2024

Dear Riyan,

Your Mom and Dad keep teaching you new things. Danielle your Nanny too is very active and keeps communicating with you. Long back you started recognising your name Riyan — when asked who is Riyan, you pointed your finger towards yourself. Since last few days “D” your Nanny showed you the letters R-I-Y-A-N and told you the spellings of your name. A fast learner as you are, you learnt it by heart. Today when your Dad left you in your crib and was watching you on the monitor, he heard you repeating R… I… Y… A… N… Riyan! You are my wonder baby. Many blessings.

Dadi
