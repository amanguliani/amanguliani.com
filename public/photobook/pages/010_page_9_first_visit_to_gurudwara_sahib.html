First Visit to Gurudwara Sahib

28 January 2023

Dear Riyan,

You are three weeks old today. We decided to take you to Gurudwara Sahib for Babaji's blessings. There is no gurudwara in close vicinity — the nearest is in Connecticut, a 40–45 minute drive. It is very cold outside. Mumma has dressed you up properly in a warm dress. Mumma and Papa have also taken out Indian attire for Gurudwara.

Bhaiji at Gurudwara made a prayer for your long, healthy and prosperous life and distributed Prasad to all of us. Papa brought two packs of chocolates for the sangat present in the gurudwara.

My little one, may Babaji bless you always.

Love,
Dadi
