Dear Riyan,

Since last few weeks we are enjoying your accelerated milestones. You have started standing up on your own and taking few steps also. We observed that you have started showing your likes and dislikes, your tastes and your preferences for certain things. As I mentioned earlier you love all your stuffed toys and have given names to each and everyone. For you they are your most loving buddies.

Your Mumma and Papa have organised your play area by putting your favourite books in an open shelf and all your stuffies in a box. You probably did not like this idea of your favourite toys being filled in a closed box. Today when Fiona your Nanny was looking after you, quietly you pulled out all the books from the shelf and placed your most loving Dinu and Sterny on that. It was a message very loud and clear that you have your own mind and personality and want to take care of the things the way you want. My dear little one it is definitely a good trait to stand unique in this world but very essential to be organised too.

Lots of love,
