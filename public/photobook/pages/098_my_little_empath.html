My little Empath (6 May 2025)

Dear Riyan,

Today afternoon after your nap you were playing with your sand pits. Suddenly it started raining outside. Dee was little uncomfortable because she is an arthritis patient and her hip joint was aching. Very casually I mentioned that this weather is not good for such patients.

You were totally engrossed in your play but still heard attentively our conversation. Both of us were overwhelmed when you said — Dee you pull a chair….sit down and take a break.

My dear little one I am so proud that you have been blessed with a loving and empathetic heart. May you always remain like this.

Love,
Dadi
