Bilingual

26 December 2024

Dear Riyan,

Nearing your 2nd birthday, you are able to speak full sentences. The good thing is that you understand that our family speaks Hindi or Punjabi and the people of America speak English. With your Nanny you communicate in English. If you want her to take you to the car wash — an activity you love — you say: “D, can we go for car wash please?”

With your Mom and Dad you speak full sentences in Hindi. When you reached LA you saw so many new things and met so many new people. You got so overwhelmed that you kept narrating those things again and again. Your Bua got so amused to see you talking in bulk — she named you CC: a Continuous Commentator! My dear cutie pie, we are your family and we love and enjoy your milestones.

Love,
Dadi
