Failure of Heating System

02 February 2023

Dear Riyan,

It was a very tough day today. The weather forecast predicted it would be extremely cold as polar winds were passing through your area. Your Papa was very cautious and made all preparations for protecting you. Suddenly in the afternoon the heating system stopped working and it started getting cold inside. Papa made emergency calls, put an extra heater in the hall and even went outside to check for blockages. The monitor was showing the temperature dropping slowly and slowly. Meanwhile Dadu wrapped you in his woollen shawl and tried to make you comfortable.

Suddenly Papa got an idea — he went to the basement and reset the heating system. It worked, and things started coming back to normal. Considering that this should not happen again, Papa called the company and scheduled to change the entire heating system of the house.

My sweetie pie, one has to be prepared for such emergencies when living in such harsh weather conditions.

Lots of love,
Dadi
