Purple crayon

31 December 2024

Dear Riyan,

You have a book which tells you about different emotions and each one of them is associated with a particular colour. You have thoroughly learnt that book as you master other books. Today evening when you came back after playing in the park with your cousins, you cried a lot. Both Aman and Nidhi tried pacifying you, offered you some food but nothing worked. After crying for a long time, finally your parents could calm you down. When you came to a position to talk, Nidhi asked you why you had cried so much — you replied that you were tired. When she asked which colour it was, you said: “Purple” — a correct answer as per the book of emotions.

My dear, books help you to convey, to communicate. You could have told your Mom and Dad that you were feeling tired instead of crying so much. Never cry, my little one.

Love,
Dadi
