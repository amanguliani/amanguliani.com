Classifying water spouts (19 Apr 2025)

Dear Riyan,

You are a very curious child who wants to know details of working of all the systems of your house. Few days back there was a heavy rainfall and one of the water spouts pushed some of the wood chips lying on the walkway with its strong water flow. From there it started the story of water spouts. Nidhi named all of them with a different name. Gradually all the 6 water spouts around the house were part of your daily story. Later your Papa added a spice to the story by respectfully naming each spout by addressing it Mr so and so.

Today morning we were all getting ready to drop your Dadu to the airport as he has to go to India. It was fine weather. You accompanied Dadu and me around the house and emulating your father told us that Riyan will tell us about each spout with putting his shoe on it.

First one is Mr Woodchip pusher….the one which pushed wood chips from the walkway during rain. Another is Mr under the soil….the reason of naming it so was because its rainwater was flooding the driveway of the neighbour’s house…so it was decided to put its extended pipe under the soil. Another Mr on the grass, then Mr on the walkway, Mr open and last Mr in the mud.

Your detailed understanding, learning the concepts with clarity and narrating them in a cute manner made both your Dadu and me awestruck.

Love you Riyan,
Dadi
