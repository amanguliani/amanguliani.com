Your Home Coming

09 January 2023

Dear Riyan,

We are so excited today as you are coming home. There is a beautiful flower bouquet and a yummy cake on the dining table waiting for you. Your bedroom is all set with a fresh sheet on the bed, your crib, your diaper changing station and all other stuff you might need. A similar set up has been made down stairs. Whenever you would like to have family time you might need them here too.

Ohh....I can hear the door of the garage opening. Your Papa brought you inside in the car seat. Dressed up in a white quilted dress and red woollen cap, you are looking so adorable. After cutting the cake, your Mumma took you to her bedroom to feed you. Simultaneously I saw your Papa put all your statistics of eating, Pooping and Peeing on the App he has downloaded on the phone. Mumma too shared the App to be on the same page.

My little cutie pie this is a tech world you have entered into. Try to learn and take all positive aspects of this modern world but discreetly keep away from its damaging factors which separates one human being from another.

Love you my darling,
Dadi
