Dadu and Dadi reached Wilbraham
15 March 2024

Dear Riyan,

Your Papa has to go to South Africa for a week as a part of his eMBA program. He didn't want to leave you and your Mom all alone here in this big house. He got our tickets booked and after taking a direct flight from Delhi we reached New York on 10th of March. Aman picked us up and drove us to 5 Kensington Drive where our little Riyan lived.

Reaching here we saw you crawling and moving with a walker. It seems that you are just ready to walk on your own. Very soon you became friendly with your Dadu and me. You are able to speak and communicate very well. As it is difficult for you to call me Dadi... you decided to call him Dada and me Da. Listening to rhymes, songs and stories is your favourite time. I too loved singing new rhymes in English as well as in Hindi. Your Mom is very busy with her research work so I took over the kitchen's management. Home made yoghurt is added to your daily meal which you have started loving.

Love, Dadi
