Changed to front facing car seat

30 September 2024

Dear Riyan,

In America a car seat for small kids is mandatory. From day one when your Mom Dad brought you from the hospital, you were tied up in a car seat facing backward. A mirror was fixed over the seat from where you could see your Mom or Dad driving the car. Initially you used to feel uncomfortable but later your parents played all your favourite songs, gave you your best stuffies, and you started enjoying even long drives. Rules permit putting the car seat forward after a particular age or if the child weighs a desired weight. You are a lean and thin baby, but today the doctor allowed you to sit in the car seat facing forward. This changed the entire scenario for you. Now not only can you see forward, but you can directly talk to Mom and Dad sitting in the front.

Love,
Dadi
