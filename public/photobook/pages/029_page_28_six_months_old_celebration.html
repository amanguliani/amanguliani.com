Six months old celebration

07 July 2023

Dear Riyan,

You are six months old today. To celebrate this very special day your Bua, Fufu, Ridam, Reet and their grandma are all with us. Devinder Dadu too flew all the way from California to meet you. Your Mom and Dad organised a lunch party at Priya restaurant — besides family, few friends also joined us there.

In the evening the same old team — Neha Masi, Surya Chachu and your Bua — were on their toes to inflate balloons, put banners, arrange cake and to gift you appropriate dresses for the day. This time they have a very enthusiastic little helper: your Reet Didi. It is a very hot day so your Fufu decided to make a grilling arrangement in the backyard.

My little darling your Dadu and I will be leaving for Delhi on 21st of this month. Ridam and Reet are your first cousins — they love you so much. Always remain connected with them.

With many hugs and kisses, my sweetie pie.
Lots of love, Dadi
