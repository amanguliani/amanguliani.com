Waiting for Nanima

16 February 2023

Dear Riyan,

Your Dadu and me have to go back to India to appear for our green card interview. I didn't want to go as you are so small and require constant care. Your Papa insisted that we should go on 20th February on our scheduled flight. Meanwhile he talked to your Nani Mumma who readily agreed to come here. She too was eagerly waiting to meet her little grandchild. Your Papa has gone to the airport to bring your Nanima home. We will leave on 20th but you will have a good time with her.

My dear little one, it is a blessing for grandparents to play and have good quality time with their grandkids.

Lots of love,
Dadi
