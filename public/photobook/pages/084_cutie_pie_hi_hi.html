Cutie pie… hi hi!

Dear Riyan,

Ridam said “Cutie pie” and you completed the sentence by saying “Hi hi!” It happened two times — then suddenly you realised that Ridam was taking the lead and you were following him. Have more fun and enjoy your LA stay.
Love,
