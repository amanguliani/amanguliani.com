Happy Birthday — 1 year!
07 January 2024

Dear Riyan,

Today you have completed one year of your life. It is your first birthday. Your Mom Dad, Masis and Chachu are all excited to make your first birthday celebration a grand one. They all joined hands and decorated the entire house with red, yellow and orange balloons. You too were made to dress up in the same colors with a Pooh T-shirt.

I as usual wrote a poem for your first birthday and sent it to you. Your Papa got framed your picture and poem and decorated in front of the banner of your name. A cake smash photo shoot has been planned for February 6th. For today Papa and Mumma invited some of their friends and ordered a beautiful cake and food from outside.

Weather today is very rough and there is a probability of a storm. Many of the invited guests could not reach for the party because of the harsh weather conditions. My dear little one, many blessings from Dadu and Dadi for your long, healthy and happy life. May you enjoy each and every moment of your life.

Love, Dadi
