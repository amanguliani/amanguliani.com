Tears flowing from Fiona's eye

22 April 2024

Dear Riyan,

Last week Fiona was on leave visiting her home town. She left you when you were crawling or walking with some support. Today it was a different scenario. Since last three days you were practising walking independently. Fiona came for her regular duty today on Monday — went to the washroom to wash her hands — came out and saw her little darling smartly walking towards her. She got so overwhelmed to see this scene… tears started flowing from her eyes. She picked you up, hugged you and kissed you. After settling down she gave you all the beautiful gifts she brought for you from Norway.

My dear Riyan, Fiona loves you a lot — you are also attached to her. You call her Ona.

Lots of love,
Dadi
