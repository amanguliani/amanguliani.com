Ek minute (25 Apr 2025)

Dear Riyan,

It has been an amazing experience since last few days when I found that anything I speak in Hindi….you pick it up and want me to repeat it again in a similar situation.

Your Crib with dozens of your stuffy friends is a piece of pride for you. You want everyone who comes to our house to see it. Few days back I volunteered to arrange all your friends in your Crib and must have used the word ek minute….you liked the arrangement and said Riyan ka Crib beautiful ho gaya. Today when I did it again you asked me to repeat the word ek minute.

In another similar incident when I was preparing to go out to see my plants, you said…Dadi Riyan ke bina mat jana…Riyan ko saath le kar jaana. I love my own words echoing from your little tongue.

Love you,
Dadi
