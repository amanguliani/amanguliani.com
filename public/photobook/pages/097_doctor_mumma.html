Doctor Mumma (5 May 2025)

Dear Riyan,

Today was your Mumma’s PhD defense. She had to give a presentation in front of her advisors, her colleagues, lab mates and family. Since last many days she was intensely working on it. Akki Masi travelled all the way from Chicago to support her, help her and to roast her presentation. Chinu, Chani and Mazi also came to attend your Mumma’s presentation. It was scheduled for 2 o’clock in the afternoon. We all got ready to reach her department before time. Dee your Nanny volunteered to bring you there.

When you reached there Nidhi was nearing to end of her presentation, acknowledging all the committee members, friends and family. You were looking very cute twinning with your father. When committee members declared your Mom to be a doctor, everyone congratulated her, opened bottles of champagne and she was asked to cut a cake. You were too enthusiastic to cut cake with your Mumma.

After the program we all went for dinner in a Mexican restaurant. You ate few chips with guacamole and told Aman that you wanted to go out and have some fresh air. Your this statement made all of us laugh because we knew that it is not the fresh air but the water spouts around the restaurant which you wanted to explore.

My dear Riyan today was a wonderful day when your Mom received her PhD degree. I wish and pray that you too achieve many such goals in your life.

Love,
Dadi
