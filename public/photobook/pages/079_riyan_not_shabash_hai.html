Riyan not Shabash hai

22 December 2024

Dear Riyan,

Your parents are grooming you with great love and care. They reward you when you follow their instructions and do a good job, and stop you from doing certain acts which can be harmful. Your Mom says “Shabash” when you do a good job — it means “well done” in Hindi. A very conscious child you are. You can differentiate between good and bad. Whenever your naughty self forces you to do an act which is prohibited, you tell yourself: “Riyan not Shabash hai.”

I am sure when you will be a big boy and will read these memories of yours, you will laugh over your cute acts.

Love,
Dadi
